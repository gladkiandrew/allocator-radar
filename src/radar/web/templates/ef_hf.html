Hedge fund buyers: the {{ rows|length }} organizations ranked by the hedge fund exposure their latest IRS Form 990 or 990-PF discloses, in dollars and as a percent of investments. Every figure cites the form, tax year, part and line, and links to the filing.

Ranking. Organizations are ranked by the hedge fund (named lines) figure first. Those without one follow, ranked by the broader alternatives (other investments) figure, then those with neither.

Hedge fund (named lines) is the sum of the other-investment lines on the latest return whose own description names a hedge fund strategy (hedge fund, absolute return, global macro, multi-strategy, market neutral, long/short, arbitrage, event driven, managed futures and similar): Form 990 Schedule D Part VII (investments, other securities) or the Form 990-PF Part II line 13 statement. A fund that is a hedge fund but is not described as one is not counted. Lines described only as "alternative investments" are not counted here either, because they can hold private equity or real estate; they are noted under the broader figure.

Alternatives (other investments) is the whole other-investments line: Form 990 Part X line 12 (investments, other securities) or Form 990-PF Part II line 13 (investments, other). It is everything the filer did not report as publicly traded securities, so it can also include commingled stock and bond funds, private equity and real estate. It is not a hedge fund figure.

Percent of investments = figure ÷ (public securities + other investments): Form 990 Part X lines 11 + 12, or Form 990-PF Part II lines 10a–10c + 13. A line left blank on the return counts as zero; when both are blank the percent is not computed. Each figure is labeled with the value it uses: fair market value (FMV) where the return gives one for that line, otherwise book value. The 990 balance sheet is at book value; the 990-PF totals are at FMV, while the 990-PF statement lines read so far carry book value only, so those percents divide a book value by an FMV total and are labeled that way.

"Not disclosed" means the return on file does not report the figure. It is never shown as zero. 990s are filed once a year and lag by a year or more.
